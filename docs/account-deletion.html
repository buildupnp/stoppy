Account Deletion

Last updated: April 1, 2026

Delete Your Account

If you created a Stoppy account, you can request deletion of your synced account data using either of the methods below.

Method 1: In the App

  1. Open Stoppy on your device.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Account.
  4. Tap Request Account Deletion.
  5. Confirm your request.

Method 2: Via Email

Send an email to hatiyaghatiya@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and include the email address associated with your Stoppy account.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your account profile data (email, display name).
  • Synced progress data stored on our servers (coins, streaks, achievements, blocked app configurations, activity history).
  • Feedback submissions associated with your account.

What May Remain on Your Device

Local app data stored on your device may remain until you clear app data or uninstall the app.

Note: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All synced data will be permanently removed from our servers.

Processing Time

Account deletion requests are typically processed within 7 business days. You will receive a confirmation email once your data has been deleted.
