# Privacy Policy for Stoppy

Last updated: April 1, 2026

## Overview

Stoppy helps users block distracting apps, track workouts, and manage focus-related progress. This policy explains what data the app uses and why.

## Data We Collect

- Account data: email address and display name when you create an account or sign in.
- App data: blocked apps, unlock sessions, coin balance, streaks, achievements, preferences, and activity history.
- Activity data: push-ups, step counts, and other in-app progress data.
- Feedback data: messages you submit through the feedback form or account deletion request flow.

## Sensitive Permissions

- Accessibility Service: used only to detect the app package you open so Stoppy can block apps you selected.
- Usage Access: used to show app usage time and related insights for apps you manage in Stoppy.
- Overlay permission: used to show the lock screen on top of blocked apps.
- Activity Recognition: used for step tracking features.
- Camera: used only during AI workout sessions for on-device pose detection.

Stoppy does not use Accessibility to read typed text, passwords, messages, or screen content.

## How We Use Data

- To provide core app features such as app blocking, unlock timers, workouts, statistics, and syncing.
- To store account-linked progress across devices.
- To respond to feedback and account deletion requests.
- To improve reliability and user support.

## Data Storage

- Some data is stored locally on your device.
- Synced account data is stored in Supabase.
- Network traffic for synced features is sent over HTTPS.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may use service providers such as Supabase and Google authentication services to operate the app.

## Your Choices

- You can disable permissions in Android settings at any time.
- You can sign out at any time.
- You can request account deletion from the app in Settings > Account > Request Account Deletion.

## Account Deletion

If you created an account, you can request deletion of your synced account data:

- In the app: Settings > Account > Request Account Deletion
- On the web: publish and link your hosted account deletion page in the Play Console
