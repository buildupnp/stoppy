# Account Deletion for Stoppy

Last updated: April 1, 2026

## Delete Your Account

If you created a Stoppy account, you can request deletion of your synced account data in either of these ways:

- In the app: open Settings > Account > Request Account Deletion
- On the web: use this public page once it is hosted

## What Gets Deleted

Your account deletion request should remove synced account data associated with your Stoppy account, including account profile data and synced progress stored by the service.

## What May Remain on Your Device

Local app data stored on your device may remain until you clear app data or uninstall the app.
